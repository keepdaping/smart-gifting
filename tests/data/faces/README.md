Test faces for the face-check tests.

`person-*.jpg` and `live-*.jpg` (straight and head-turned photos of three people) come from
the AT&T "Database of Faces" (formerly the ORL database), AT&T Laboratories Cambridge, collected
1992-1994 with the subjects' consent for research use. They are volunteers, not public figures.
Source: https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/dtg/attarchive/facedatabase.html
