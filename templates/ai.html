Component 1

Gift Recommender

Type a person or an occasion in plain words and get matching gifts from every store.

  1. Read the request

    “birthday gift for my mum under 50k” is cleaned into words, and the budget (UGX 50,000) is picked out.

  2. Match by words (TF-IDF)

    Each gift's name, description and tags are scored for the words you typed, weighting rare words higher.

  3. Match by meaning (LSA)

    Latent Semantic Analysis links related words, so “sweet treat” can find ice cream even without the word “sweet”.

  4. Rank within budget

    The two scores are blended (the hybrid model) and gifts over the budget are removed.

Component 2

Fraud Check at the Till

Every time a cashier redeems a voucher, an Isolation Forest model scores how unusual the redemption looks and warns the cashier when it stands out.

What it looks at

  • How soon after purchase it is redeemed
  • Redemptions from the same device in the last hour
  • Vouchers the same buyer bought in 24 hours
  • Wrong codes tried before this one
  • Time of day
  • Amount, compared with what that store usually sells

Isolation Forest learns what normal redemptions look like and flags the ones that are easy to isolate from the rest. Unlike fixed rules, it catches combinations of signals that are each only slightly odd.
